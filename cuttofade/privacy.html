The short version Your scripts are yours. We don't collect any data. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Scripts are saved on your device and synced through your own private iCloud, which we can't access.

This policy covers Cut to Fade for iPhone, iPad and Mac (“the app”), published by Tachena under the name Chie (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

None. We don't run servers for the app, and it never sends your scripts, titles, notes or anything else to us or to any third party. We have no way to read your writing.

Where your scripts are stored

Scripts, drafts and collections are saved on your device first, so you can write offline. The app also keeps them in your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), so every iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to your Apple Account stays in sync. That database belongs to your Apple Account and counts toward your iCloud storage. We can't access it, and neither can anyone else. Apple handles it under the Apple Privacy Policy.

  • If you aren't signed in to iCloud, your scripts stay on that device only.
  • Deleted scripts go to Recently Deleted for 30 days, then are removed from the device and from iCloud.
  • Some settings are kept per device and never synced, such as page appearance, text size and “Continue writing”.

Importing and exporting

The app opens only the files you choose, such as a Fountain or text file from Files, Mail or AirDrop. PDF and Fountain exports are created on your device and go wherever you send them through the share sheet or save panel. On the Mac, the app can access only files you pick, and uses the network only for iCloud sync.

Purchases

Cut to Fade Pro is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple gives developers only anonymous, aggregated sales figures.

Tips

The app shows a one-time tip about the format bar using Apple's TipKit. Whether you've seen it is recorded on your device only.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code, and it doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you've chosen to share crash data with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix bugs.

Children

The app is a writing tool for general audiences and is not aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
