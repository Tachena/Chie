Privacy Policy

Sky Level for iPhone

Last updated September 28, 2026

The short version

Sky Level doesn't collect any data. There's no account, no ads and no tracking. Your readings, settings and saved references stay on your iPhone. If you let Sky Level use your location, it's used only on your device to work out sunrise and sunset, and it never leaves your phone.

Information we collect

None. Sky Level has no servers and no account system, and it doesn't send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

Motion sensors

Sky Level reads your iPhone's motion sensors to measure tilt and show the level. Readings are used live on your device while the app is open. They aren't recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Location

Location is optional. If you choose "Use Current Location" in Settings, Sky Level asks iOS for your approximate location so the background sky can follow sunrise and sunset where you are. Specifically:

  • Approximate location is enough, and it's what iOS offers by default.
  • Sunrise and sunset are calculated on your iPhone. Your location is never sent to us or to any other service.
  • Your location is kept only in memory while the app is running. It isn't saved to storage.
  • Location is requested only while you're using the app. There's no background or continuous tracking.

If you decline, Sky Level uses the manual sky times you set instead. You can change this at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Settings and saved references

Your preferences, such as units, tolerance and sky choice, and any named references you save are stored only in the app's storage on your iPhone. They may be included in your device backups, which are handled by Apple under your own backup settings. Deleting Sky Level removes them.

Purchases

Sky Level Pro is an in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple Account information. Apple gives developers only anonymous, aggregated sales figures. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code, and it doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you've chosen to share crash data with app developers, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports, which we use only to fix bugs.

Children

Sky Level is a measuring tool for general audiences and is not aimed at children. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
