Frequently asked questions

What's free, and what does Pro add?

The level is free: surface and spirit modes, degrees, slope percent and mm per metre, Set Zero, Lock, precision mode, the living sky, and up to 3 saved references. Sky Level Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds target angles, trade units (roof pitch, inches per foot, ratio), proximity haptics, more skies including your own colors, and unlimited saved references.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in the app (the slider button, top right), tap Unlock Sky Level Pro, then Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can my family use Pro?

Yes. Sky Level Pro works with Family Sharing, so members of your Family Sharing group get it too. They can tap Restore Purchases if it doesn't appear right away.

My readings are slightly off. How do I fix that?

A phone case or the camera bump can tilt your iPhone a little when it's lying flat. Put the phone on a surface you know is level, let it settle, and tap Set Zero. Readings are then measured from that position. Use the same case and the same edge when you measure. Reset goes back to true gravity.

What's the difference between Surface and Spirit mode?

Surface mode is for a phone lying screen-up on a table or shelf and shows tilt in two directions. Spirit mode is for a phone standing on one of its edges, like a traditional spirit level. Sky Level switches between them automatically as you move the phone; you can fix one in Settings › Mode.

How do I check an angle like 45°?

With Pro, turn on Target angle in Settings › Pro tools and choose the angle. Stand the phone on its edge in Spirit mode: the level cue, tap and chime fire when you reach that angle, on either side.

What do the units mean?

Tap any reading to cycle through units. Slope % is rise over run times 100. mm/m is millimetres of rise per metre. Roof pitch shows rise per 12 of run, so 4 / 12 is a 4-in-12 roof. Inches per foot rounds to the nearest 1/16 in. Ratio shows 1 unit of rise per N of run, for example 1 : 48 for drainage.

The screen won't turn sideways.

Turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center. Spirit mode works on either long edge in landscape.

Why would Sky Level use my location?

Only if you ask it to. With the sky set to Automatic, tapping Use Current Location in Settings lets the background follow sunrise and sunset where you are. It uses approximate location, works it out on your iPhone, and never sends it anywhere. Without it, the sky follows the manual times in Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.

I can't hear the level sound.

Turn on Hands-free level sound in Settings. The chime follows your ring switch, so it's silent when your iPhone is in Silent Mode.

The screen keeps turning off while I measure.

Check that Keep screen on is turned on in Settings. It keeps the display awake while Sky Level is open.
