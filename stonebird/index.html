The story

The raid came at dawn. Tendai Moyo, the plateau's greatest test pilot, the one they called Stone Bird, went down defending the range. Her daughter Farai escaped in the one thing they couldn't take: her mother's prototype jet, Goredema.

Now Farai flies for whoever pays, taking contracts from the trading houses that run the skies of an alternate 1990s Africa, and asking who really ordered the raid.

The game

A vertical-scrolling air-combat game in the tradition of the classic arcade shooters, told as a story.

Fly a story

A cinematic campaign told through radio chatter, set pieces and boss fights. Escort river convoys, break a desert gun line, outfly an enemy ace.

Build your loadout

Earn on every contract and spend it in Nyeleni's hangar. Switch heavy weapons in flight: the Sun Lance beam, the Mosi plasma cannon, the Hammerhead missile.

Restore Goredema

Your mother's jet came out of the raid damaged and locked down. Wake her engine stage by stage and bring her targeting computer back online.

A world of its own

River forests, sandstone plateaus and the open erg in dust storms. Brass-and-gold aircraft, an original score, and no ads.
