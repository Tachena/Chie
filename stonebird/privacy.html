Stone Bird

The Contract Era · Privacy Policy

In short: Stone Bird collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress is saved only on your own device, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

This policy explains how Stone Bird: The Contract Era (“the game”), made by Chie Collective, handles information. It applies to the iOS game and to beta versions distributed through TestFlight.

What the game stores

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves a small amount of game data on your device:

  • your campaign progress, money, weapons, upgrades and supplies;
  • your settings, such as music, sound effect and radio voice volume.

This data stays on your iPhone. It is not sent to us, is not linked to your identity, and is deleted when you delete the game. It may be included in your device’s own iCloud or computer backup, which is handled by Apple under your backup settings.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours.
  • It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • It contains no in-app purchases.

TestFlight beta testing

If you test a beta version through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple may share certain information with us so we can improve the game: the feedback, comments and screenshots you choose to send, crash reports, and basic details such as your device model and iOS version. If you were invited by email, we also see the name and email address you were invited with. We use this only to fix problems and improve the game, and we do not sell or share it. How Apple itself handles TestFlight data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes how information is handled (for example, by adding online features), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date below will change.
