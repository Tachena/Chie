Privacy Policy

Sun Sizer Pro & Sun Sizer

ChiePrivacySupport

Effective September 23, 2026

The short version Sun Sizer doesn't collect, store or share any of your personal data. There is no account, no cloud and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

This policy covers the iOS and iPadOS apps Sun Sizer Pro and Sun Sizer (together, “the apps”), published by Tachena (“we”, “us”).

Information we collect

None. We do not operate servers for the apps, and the apps do not send your projects, measurements, location or any other information to us or to anyone else.

Data stored on your device

Projects, equipment libraries, measurements and settings are saved locally on your iPhone or iPad. They are included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backups if you have backups turned on, and those backups are managed by Apple under your Apple account settings. Deleting the app removes this data from the device.

Location

If you tap Use my location and grant permission, the app reads your device's location to fill in a site's latitude and longitude for sun-position and climate calculations. The location is used on the device only and is saved only inside the project you're working on. It is never transmitted. You can deny or revoke location access at any time in Settings and type coordinates in by hand instead.

Camera and motion sensors

Measurement tools use the device's motion sensors and, where offered, the camera to estimate tilt, facing and surface size. Readings are processed on the device in real time. The apps don't save or upload photos or video.

Climate data

Sunlight and temperature figures come from a NASA POWER dataset bundled inside the app. Looking up a site doesn't contact NASA or any other service.

PDF reports and sharing

When you export a report, the app creates a PDF on your device and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes next, such as Mail, Messages or Files, is your choice and is handled by the app you pick.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email, payment details or Apple account information. We may see anonymous, aggregated sales figures that Apple provides to all developers.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

The apps contain no advertising, no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and do not track you across other companies' apps or websites. If you have opted in to share crash data with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These contain no personal information and are used only to fix bugs.

Children

The apps are professional tools and are not directed to children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how the apps handle data, we'll update this page and the effective date above before that version is released.

Contact
